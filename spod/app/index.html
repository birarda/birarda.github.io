MENU

Drag around the wheel to scroll, tap the center to select, tap MENU to go back, hold it to switch the backlight. Pick a song (or Shuffle Songs) to get to Now Playing, where the wheel sets the volume, the center button switches to scrubbing, and holding it opens Speakers. Keyboard: ↑ ↓ Enter Esc (hold for backlight), Space play/pause, ← → previous/next, H the Hold switch (on the top edge).
Library and speakers are sample data, not your Spotify or Sonos, and nothing makes a sound. Sign in to Spotify below to use your own.

Once you’ve agreed on Spotify’s page, it goes to spod’s relay page, which can’t bring you back here. Copy the whole address from the address bar, or the one the relay shows, even if the page doesn’t load, and paste it here.

Signed in: Music has your playlists and albums, and songs play through Spotify Connect, on the speaker chosen in Extras → Speakers, or Settings → sPod → Speakers in purist mode.
